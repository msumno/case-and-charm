# Case & Charm

## About Our Business
Case & Charm is a customizable phone case business created for our CIS-385 final project. Our business allows customers to personalize their phone cases and choose accessories that match their individual style.

We offer customizable phone cases and bundles that include a phone case, charm, and keychain. Our goal is to provide customers with a fun and affordable way to express their personalities through everyday accessories.

## Project Goals
The main goal of this project is to create a functional business website while applying the information systems and technology concepts we have learned in CIS-385.

Our objectives include:
- Creating an attractive and user-friendly website.
- Allowing customers to explore our products and customization options.
- Developing a preorder form for customers.
- Connecting customer orders to an Airtable database.
- Using Google Analytics 4 to track website traffic and customer engagement.
- Promoting our products through Instagram.
- Working together as a team to manage and complete the project.

## Technology and Tools
We are using several tools to develop and manage our business:

- **Website:** Displays our products and provides customers with preorder options.
- **Airtable:** Organizes customer information and preorder submissions.
- **Google Analytics 4:** Tracks website traffic and customer interactions.
- **Instagram:** Promotes our products and directs customers to our website.
- **GitHub:** Helps our team organize project tasks, document progress, and collaborate.

## Marketing Strategy
Our primary marketing strategy focuses on Instagram. We will create promotional posts featuring our customizable phone cases, different designs, and available bundles.

Customers will be encouraged to click the link in our Instagram bio to visit our website and preorder their personalized products.
